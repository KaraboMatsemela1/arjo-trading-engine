# Acquisition Boundary

`ACQUISITION_TOOLING_READY` authorizes replayable source acquisition only.

It does not authorize strategy interpretation, executable predicate synthesis, trade counting, performance inspection, backtesting, optimization, paper execution, or live execution.

Captured payloads may support later evidence work only when the manifest proves direct first-party contact and the payload is SHA-256 bound. Fixture, locator-only, secondary, or transport-failure records receive zero semantic closure credit.
