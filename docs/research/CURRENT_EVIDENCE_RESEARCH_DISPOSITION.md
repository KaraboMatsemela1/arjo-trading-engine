## Why another strategy is not being invented

The candidate audit reviewed 36 admitted concepts. The remaining named or partial concepts do not currently define a complete deterministic trigger/execution family. Important examples include Order Block/MT, Order Flow/Target/Bias, MMBM/LRLR, Market Maker Models, Market Structure, Third Candle, Time and Price and SMT.

The two unrepresented operational cues from the omission audit were independently dispositioned: LRLR is descriptive without a bounded executable transition, and Tape Reading is explicitly observational rather than an entry method.

Using generic ICT/SMC definitions to fill these gaps would change the research question from “does the admitted first-party Arjo strategy work?” to “can we invent a nearby strategy after seeing prior failures?” That is prohibited.

1. new direct first-party evidence closes a currently incomplete strategy family without generic-market inference;
2. a genuinely independent family is deterministically specified and preregistered before any outcome access; or

A future lane must begin with semantics and preregistration. It must not begin by mining the consumed V3-C ledger or loosening a failed family until historical results improve.
