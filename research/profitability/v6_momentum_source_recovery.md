## First-party semantics used

1. **Market Structure -> Order Flow -> Candle Science**
   - https://tradingmmt.com/market-structure-order-flow-candle-science/
   - Market Structure supplies the main direction; recent Order Flow/FVG legs refine continuation location and the price area that should not be retraced; Candle Science supplies continuation timing.

2. **The “Momentum” Theory**
   - https://tradingmmt.com/the-momentum-theory/
   - Momentum is directional speed; FVGs express momentum. Opposing FVGs on the same timeframe or one timeframe lower are explicit disturbance signs rather than automatic reversal proof.

3. **The 3 Cycles of Momentum**
   - https://tradingmmt.com/the-3-cycles-of-momentum/
   - With a still-valid HTF target, the sequence is disturbance -> consolidation/retracement -> expansion. Momentum building into expansion is identified by retracements becoming progressively smaller.

4. **The Best LTF Entries Happen Here**
   - https://tradingmmt.com/the-best-ltf-entries-happen-here/
   - LTF entries are preferred when the HTF is ready to expand. At consolidation extremes, large retracements indicate continued consolidation; smaller retracements indicate impending expansion.

5. **The Easiest Areas to Trade**
   - https://tradingmmt.com/the-easiest-areas-to-trade/
   - ITH/ITL are described as liquidity magnets. An ITL is a Swing Low with higher Swing Lows on each side; ITH is the inverse.

6. **The Closer the DOL The Better Your Trade is**
   - https://tradingmmt.com/the-closer-the-dol-the-better-your-trade-is/
   - A closer Draw on Liquidity / target is expected to pull price faster; the last AoO before the DOL occurs after the final blocking PD Array is disrespected.
